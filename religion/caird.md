# G. B. Caird: Saint Luke

- pg. 53:

> The Latin Vulgate version of Gabriel's salutation to Mary -- 'Hail Mary, full of grace' -- has contributed to the veneration paid to Mary by her devotees, on the ground that she is able to dispense to others from the plenitude of grace which she in her own right possesses. The Greek, however, does not admit of any such interpretation.

One does rather wonder, then, why the Eastern churches hold Mary in such high esteem.
